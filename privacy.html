Factcheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-20

Factcheck is a privacy-first writing assistant.

What we process

  • Draft text entered in the app.
  • Optional worker requests when the user enables worker mode.
  • Minimal product analytics events without user text content.

What we do not process

  • Audio recordings are never uploaded.
  • Worker logs do not store user text content.
